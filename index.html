FAQ

How much space does the booth need?

About 8×8 feet, plus access to a standard electrical outlet within 25 feet.

Which areas do you serve?

Peekskill, Cortlandt, Yorktown, Putnam County, Westchester County and the wider Hudson Valley. Travel is free within 25 miles of Peekskill; $50 for 25 to 50 miles, then $1 per mile.

How far in advance should I book?

Popular dates (especially spring and fall weekends) fill up fast — we recommend booking 2–6 months ahead.

Can the prints be customized?

Yes! We design a custom print template with your names, date, logo or event theme.

Is a deposit required?

Yes, a $150 deposit holds your date and counts toward your total. It is fully refundable if you cancel more than 14 days before your event, and non-refundable if you cancel within 14 days. If we can’t offer your date, or we have to cancel, you get a full refund. The balance is due on or before your event date.

Can you set up outdoors?

Yes, with a covered, level area and access to power. Let us know your venue details.

When do you arrive to set up?

We typically arrive about an hour before booth time so everything is ready when guests are.

Ready to Book Your Date?

Pick your package, tell us about your event and hold your date with a $150 deposit. We reply within 24 hours.
