Silver or Gold?

Both include an on-site attendant and unlimited instant prints. Gold doubles the time to 6 hours (Silver is 3), adds the red carpet and upgrades to a premium backdrop. Choose Silver for parties and short events; choose Gold for weddings and long evenings. Gold also costs less per hour (about $142 an hour, versus $183 for Silver).

Sales tax may apply. A $150 deposit holds your date (refund rules).
