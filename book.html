Silver or Gold? Both include an on-site attendant and unlimited instant prints. Gold doubles the time to 6 hours (Silver is 3), adds the red carpet and upgrades to a premium backdrop. Choose Silver for parties and short events; choose Gold for weddings and long evenings. Gold also costs less per hour (about $142 an hour, versus $183 for Silver).

Need a small party setup? We also offer a separate drop-off booth with no attendant: $299 for 3 hours. Sales tax may apply. A $150 deposit holds your date (refund rules).

Choose your package & send your details

Pick Bronze, Silver or Gold in the form below.

Form not showing? Open the booking form in a new tab.

First-time booker with a promo code? Enter it in the form’s promo code field. A valid first-time code takes $25 off your package total (not the $150 deposit); we apply it when we confirm your final price.

Deposit and refunds: sending the form does not reserve your date, and there is no automatic availability guarantee. Your date is reserved only once we confirm your booking through HoneyBook. The $150 deposit is paid separately through Square and counts toward your total. It is fully refundable if you cancel more than 14 days before your event, and non-refundable within 14 days. If your date turns out to be taken, or we have to cancel, we refund everything you paid. The balance is due on or before your event date. Full details are in our Terms.
